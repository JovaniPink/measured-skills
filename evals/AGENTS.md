# Evaluation guidance

- Keep committed fixtures synthetic or public and safe to publish.
- Preserve positive, near-boundary, safety, output-quality, and baseline distinctions.
- Treat repeated executions as stability evidence, not independent cases.
- Do not encode private product prompts, identifiers, failure patterns, or raw traces.
- Structural fixtures do not prove installed-client behavior.
- `plugin-eval` runs are paid and need the maintainer's yes for each arm. Keep raw results out of the repository.
