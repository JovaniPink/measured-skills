# Decision Map

Use a decision map for work that spans several sessions or depends on unresolved choices.

For each decision record:

- Stable identifier and question.
- Status: proposed, ratified, rejected, or unresolved.
- Decision owner and required evidence.
- Options and consequences.
- Dependencies on earlier decisions.
- Work that is blocked or invalidated by the outcome.
- Review trigger and latest verified date.

The map describes decision structure. It must not promote a proposal to a ratified decision or use task progress as evidence of approval.
