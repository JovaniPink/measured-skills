# Editing checklist

Use this focused pass when the draft needs more than a sentence-level cleanup.

## Meaning and evidence

- Preserve the exact claim status: observed, tested, proposed, approved, deployed, live, estimated, or inferred.
- Keep direct sources near the claims they support.
- Replace vague scale words with measured units or name the missing measurement.
- Keep uncertainty when the evidence does not justify precision.

## Structure and voice

- Put the decision or conclusion before process narration.
- Give each paragraph one job and make its first sentence carry that job.
- Prefer concrete nouns and active verbs, but retain passive voice when the actor is unknown or irrelevant.
- Remove headings that merely repeat the next sentence.
- Preserve useful emphasis and individual cadence instead of forcing uniform short sentences.

## Final check

Read only the revised version. Confirm that it still sounds like the named author or publication, that no fact became stronger, and that every remaining sentence changes the reader's understanding.
