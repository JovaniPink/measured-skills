## Residual observations and a stopping decision

The answers are not perfect. They are longer than needed, and some requests for
platform information are irrelevant to the supplied case. The native GCP answer
initially calls `ingested_at` an arrival date, then explicitly leaves correction
arrival versus original ingestion unknown. The review labels an equivalently
protected change a readability regression without establishing that judgment.
Continuity also treats obtaining missing repository context as scope expansion;
an ordinary authorized repository review should inspect relevant callers without
inventing another approval checkpoint.

These observations do not invalidate the prepared checks: no correction window,
false test outcome, unconditional disclosure finding or unauthorized action
followed. Keep them as usability observations, not newly invented release gates.
Do not start another identical wording retest without new material evidence.

Four known cases cannot establish reliable activation, causal improvement, full
profile acceptance or cross-client parity. This set did not test changed-goal
continuity, explicit-only controls, installation/update/removal/rollback, app or
account libraries, or another machine. Move next to those distinct requirements
in the [release ledger](../release-readiness-2026-10-26.md). Source freshness was
not checked online during this run. No canonical, generated, catalog, frozen-study,
accepted-originality or manifest bytes changed in this observation.
