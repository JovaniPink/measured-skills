# Proposed originality review contract

Status: proposed; maintainer acceptance not recorded. Release qualification remains held. This contract does not replace the historical undefined corpus gate until the maintainer accepts an explicit disposition.

## Review scope

Pin the full source revision and file inventory. Review canonical skills, focused references, adapters, and packaged bytes separately from historical source records. Keep raw authoring sessions, private source mappings, and any restricted material outside public artifacts. Use owned history, retained license receipts, and primary authorities; do not download or compare third-party skill collections to manufacture a pass.

## Evidence classes

- Four owned adaptations: verify the owned-origin ancestor, canonical entrypoint and license hashes, recorded changes, and preserved original declarations. The current correction supports this lineage; it is not proof of universal originality.
- Forty corrected process declarations: the [retained authoring review](clean-room-declaration-review-2026-10-20.md) records literal writes and prior exposure across six creation batches. Current labels declare original authoring after exposure. Review expression, source use, and licensing independently; do not infer separation or comprehensive clearance from the correction. Preserve historical facts and raw-record access limits.
- Forty-three previously original declarations, in addition to the 40 corrections: inspect authoring history, source use, and licensing basis. Original authorship after exposure is distinct from a clean-room claim. No absence-of-exposure conclusion follows from an exact-file hash nonmatch.
- Primary-source references: distinguish a correctness or format authority from an implementation source. Check license and copied-expression questions where applicable. A citation alone does not settle them.

## Decision record

The reviewer records identity, review date, exact revision and paths, evidence inspected, classification findings, exceptions, and limitations. Each item receives `supported`, `needs_evidence`, or `requires_correction`, with a reason. Missing evidence remains missing; there is no automatic pass from age, passing tests, or absence of attribution phrases.

The maintainer then records `accepted`, `rejected`, or `needs_evidence` for the scoped disposition, with the remaining risk and re-review triggers. An independent review must be distinct from the original authoring assertion and identify conflicts or limits. If a corpus comparison is still required, specify its lawful and permitted corpus, method, thresholds, and known blind spots before execution; no such comparison is authorized here.

Acceptance cannot certify universal non-infringement, all possible prior exposure, or behavioral benefit. It can support only the stated evidence and scope. Safety, source freshness, receiving-client acceptance, merge, and release decisions remain separate.
